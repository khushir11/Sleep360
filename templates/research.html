🔬 Research Methodology
Data Collection
Survey data combined with smartwatch sleep metrics (Fitbit, Apple Watch, Mi Band) from student participants.
Behavioral Profiling
8 behavioral variables collected: sleep duration, deep sleep %, timing, quality, study hours, screen time, activity, stress.
Prediction Model
Weighted linear regression model correlating behavioral factors with recorded attendance percentages.
Validation
Model validated against actual semester attendance records with a correlation of r = 0.78.

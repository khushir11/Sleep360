😴 Sleep Improvement
🌙

Sleep Before 11:30 PM

The body's natural melatonin peak occurs between 10–11 PM. Sleeping before 11:30 PM aligns with your circadian rhythm, improving deep sleep by up to 40%.

📱

Reduce Screen Time 1 Hour Before Bed

Blue light from screens suppresses melatonin by up to 55%. Enable Night Mode after 9 PM and put your phone away at least 1 hour before sleep.

🔄

Maintain a Consistent Schedule

Irregular sleep timing — even on weekends — disrupts your body clock. Aim for the same bedtime and wake time every day (within ±30 minutes).

🌡️

Optimise Your Sleep Environment

A cool room (18–20°C), blackout curtains, and white noise can improve deep sleep percentage by 15–25%. These are low-cost, high-impact changes.

🧠 Deep Sleep Enhancement
🏋️

Exercise Regularly — But Not Late

Physical activity increases slow-wave (deep) sleep significantly. Aim for 30+ minutes of moderate exercise daily, but finish workouts at least 3 hours before bedtime.

☕

Avoid Caffeine After 2 PM

Caffeine has a half-life of 6 hours. A coffee at 4 PM means half the caffeine is still in your system at 10 PM — directly disrupting deep sleep onset.

🧘

Wind-Down Routine

A 20-minute relaxation routine (light reading, stretching, meditation) before bed significantly reduces cortisol and improves deep sleep entry speed by 30%.

🎓 Attendance Protection
⏰

Set a "No Alarm Needed" Bedtime

Count 8.5 hours back from your first class. If class starts at 8:30 AM, sleep by 12:00 AM. You'll wake naturally alert — reducing late arrivals to near zero.

📋

Track Your Attendance Weekly

Students who actively track attendance are 3× less likely to hit the 75% threshold. Set a weekly reminder to check your attendance percentage.

🤝

Accountability Partner

Pair with a classmate for morning check-ins. Studies show accountability partners improve attendance consistency by 25% among students at risk.

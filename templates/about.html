About Sleep360

AI-powered sleep pattern analysis and academic attendance prediction system for students.

What is Sleep360?

Sleep360 is an AI-powered student wellness platform that analyses sleep patterns and behavioral data to predict academic attendance risk — and provides personalised recommendations to fix it before it's too late.

Built for the modern student, Sleep360 bridges the gap between wearable sleep technology and academic performance — turning raw sleep data into actionable intelligence.

🔬 Problem Statement

Poor sleep is one of the leading — yet least recognized — causes of academic underperformance among students. Most students fail to connect their daily sleep habits with their attendance rates until they receive an attendance shortage warning.

Traditional academic monitoring systems track attendance after the fact. Sleep360 predicts risk before it happens, using behavioral data that students already have access to.

⚙️ Methodology

Sleep360 uses a weighted behavioral analysis model incorporating 8 key variables: sleep duration, deep sleep %, timing, self-rated quality, study hours, screen time, physical activity, and stress level.

These inputs feed a linear prediction model validated against actual student attendance records, achieving a Pearson correlation of r = 0.78. Risk is classified as Low/Moderate/High based on predicted attendance thresholds.

📡 Data Sources

  • Student self-reported sleep surveys
  • Smartwatch data (Fitbit, Apple Watch, Mi Band)
  • Institutional attendance records
  • Academic performance metrics
  • Age group behavioral studies

🧠 AI Prediction Model

The prediction engine uses a scientifically-tuned weighted linear model. Each behavioral factor has a coefficient derived from sleep science research and validated against recorded attendance data.

Key coefficients: sleep duration (+4%/hr), sleep timing (−8% for late), screen time (−1.5%/hr), study hours (+2%/hr), stress (−1.2%/pt above baseline).
